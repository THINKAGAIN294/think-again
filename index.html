About My Writings

Welcome to a space where curiosity meets courage. Here, I write to question, reflect, and imagine. From bold critiques of blind faith and social norms to thoughtful explorations of science, politics, and human behavior—my aim is simple: to encourage thinking before believing.

You’ll find fictional stories that spark the imagination, essays that challenge outdated traditions, rational takes on health and science, and observations on the ever-evolving world we live in. I write not to preach, but to provoke thought. To ask: What if we looked deeper? What if we used our minds before our mouths?

Why I Write

Hi, I’m Rudra. While journeying from class 10 to graduation, I realized something that pushed me to start writing. Fake news, blind faith, and hatred have quietly—but severely—crept into our society. Before 10th grade, it felt like I lived in a different world. But with the rise of social media and instant communication, misinformation now spreads faster than truth.

At first, I ignored it. But when I saw even educated people—teachers, mentors—falling into these traps, becoming biased or immoral, I couldn’t stay silent. I witnessed this especially during my college years.

So through my small writings, I don’t claim to teach—but I do humbly ask you to think again. About what you hear, what you see on your phone, and what you believe.

Thank you.
