Sorry Messi

October 6, 2026

I enjoy many sports but I closely follow a few.and you are the reason i started following one of the most entertaining sports in the world.Not knowing who you were,how good you played ,where you played and where you belong i had some notion about you.where that came from?I dont know,the internet may be.but yeah,i dont know why when I saw the advertisement of fifa world cup 2022 and being a victim of curiosity I gave it a shot . I chose you.i did not know your journey,i did not know your history,i did not know that the whole world out there was waiting for that golden little thing in your hands ,i did not know.But still my heart prayed for your victory.i still regret ,how i could not understand what was going on the pitch and went to sleep out of boredom,but why the first thing i checked in the morning was the scoreboard and a smile on the face seeing what it showed.Goosebumps as i am typing.Later after months when i came to know stuff,understand things ,i knew what it actually meant to the world.what it meant to you and your mates and your Family.Today it seems like you were there in my heart a long before i watched football,as if i was supposed to follow you,i was destined to be one of your millions fans.when i watched the story ,the tears ,the efforts ,the joy ,the roars when you opened the score against Mexico,the impossible assist to Molina against Dutch defenders ,the Gvardiol Turn ,i get jealous of those who enjoyed this cinema live that time.i wished i could have seen it.they call you god of football and you full filled my wish.i watched you win copa and i watched you this world cup.what i watched ,i watched.what i experienced in the late nights ,i experienced.The moments you have given me these years are priceless for me.

But going through these memorable scenes I had forgotten an important part of our relationship,that you have become my Idol.that i respect what you do,your values ,your advice and the type of person you are .I aspire to be like you ,I wish to be as competent as you are . You have always shown how much hard work it takes to reach there ,how patient you need to be to achieve what you wish for.you are a perfect example of how bad time shapes you ,how time can take turns.For twenty years you have been playing football better than anyone existing in our solar system,for twenty years.It teaches us about discipline.And many more ,we can learn many more seeing you,from your career ,from your character.

But I am sorry.I am sorry that these 3-4 years, accepting you my idol,i still failed somewhere to apply these lessons in my life ,the lessons you always teach us.Being your fan ,there should be no place for unnecessary hate,aggression or ego in our life.I am sorry for that.

But I promise you today,it is the day you leave the national team,and I will make this day to leave all my Vices behind.I must try to be a better person in life.thank you for leading us way in every step.

Thank you for entertaining the world for many years.Thank you for giving me many moments of joy.May God make you always this happy.

Gracias por todo, Leo. 🐐🇦🇷

R.P MOHAPATRA
